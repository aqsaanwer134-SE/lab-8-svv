# Defect Log: PSVS-REQ-v1.0 (S1-S12)

| Defect ID | Req ID | Category | Description | Evidence | Severity | Suggested fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | S1 | Incompleteness | "Registered" is not defined: who registers depositors, with what criteria, and what happens on suspension or de-registration? | "only from registered depositors" | High | Add a registration requirement (registry, approving authority, de-registration rules). | Open |
| D02 | S1 | Ambiguity | "Accept" could mean physically receive, record in the system, or approve for storage. | "shall accept seed boxes" | Medium | Define deposit as a process: receive, verify depositor, inspect, assign chamber, record. | Open |
| D03 | S2 | Vagueness | "Large number" has no measurable value. | "can hold a large number of boxes" | High | Delete S2 or replace it with the concrete capacities of S3. | Open |
| D04 | S2, S3 | Redundancy | S2 is a vague restatement of S3's capacity limits. | S2 vs S3 | Low | Merge into one capacity requirement. | Open |
| D05 | S3 | Incompleteness | No behaviour when a chamber is full; the chamber set is not defined although the scenario says "several" chambers. | "at most 50,000... 20,000" | High | State that deposit is rejected or redirected when full; define the chamber set. | Open |
| D06 | S3 | Ambiguity | Unclear what counts toward capacity: withdrawn, quarantined or in-transit boxes, or different box sizes. | "hold at most ... boxes" | Medium | Define "box slot"; count only physically present standard boxes. | Open |
| D07 | S4 | Incompleteness | Ownership transfer is not addressed (depositor merges, dissolves or is de-registered). | "belongs to exactly one depositor" | Medium | Add an ownership-transfer rule with authority approval and audit entry. | Open |
| D08 | S4, S5 | Contradiction | S4 says only the owner may withdraw; S5 lets the authority release any box to any party. | "only that depositor may withdraw" vs "release any box to any requesting party" | Critical | Make S4 "except as provided in S5" and constrain S5 (see D09, D10). | Open |
| D09 | S5 | Vagueness | "Emergency", "vault authority" and "any requesting party" are undefined, which is a security and abuse risk. | "In an emergency... any box to any requesting party" | Critical | Define emergency criteria, the authority's identity, declaration procedure and eligible recipients. | Open |
| D10 | S5, S10, S11 | Incompleteness | Effect of an emergency release on the 20% quota, redeposit, owner notification and ownership is unspecified. | S5 silent on S10/S11 | High | State whether emergency releases count in S10; require logging, owner notification and S11 on return. | Open |
| D11 | S4, S5, S10, S11 | Inconsistent terminology | "Withdraw" and "release" both mean taking a box out; "depositor" vs "requesting party" are not distinguished. | "withdraw" (S4, S10, S11) vs "release" (S5) | Medium | Create a glossary; use "removal" with subtypes (owner withdrawal, emergency release). | Open |
| D12 | S6 | Vagueness | "Safe temperature" is not quantified. | "stored at a safe temperature" | High | Specify a temperature range with tolerance, or reference S7. | Open |
| D13 | S6, S7 | Redundancy | S6 is the general form of S7. | S6 vs S7 | Low | Keep S7 as the refined requirement and trace S6 to it. | Open |
| D14 | S7, S8 | Contradiction | S7 forbids any rise above -18°C; S8 allows warming to 0°C. | "never rise above -18°C" vs "may be warmed to 0°C" | Critical | Rewrite S7: "except during an authorised maintenance window per S8". | Open |
| D15 | S7 | Infeasibility | "Never" cannot be guaranteed under power or equipment failure. | "shall never rise above" | High | Replace with an achievable target (backup power, alarm threshold, maximum excursion time). | Open |
| D16 | S7 | Incompleteness | No lower bound, tolerance, sensor, measurement frequency or response to an excursion. | "Chamber temperature" | High | Add a range, monitoring period and alarm/escalation actions. | Open |
| D17 | S8 | Incompleteness | No rule on who authorises maintenance, its duration, or what happens to the boxes inside. | "may be warmed to 0°C" | High | Require authorisation, a maximum duration and relocation of boxes before warming. | Open |
| D18 | S6, S8 | Contradiction | Warming to 0°C may not be a "safe temperature" for stored seeds. | S6 vs S8 | High | Relocate boxes to another chamber at a safe temperature during maintenance. | Open |
| D19 | S9 | Vagueness | "Low viability" and "promptly" have no thresholds. | "low viability... promptly" | Medium | Define a viability threshold per species and a time limit. | Open |
| D20 | S9 | Non-verifiability | No way is given to measure viability or confirm that flagging happened in time. | "flagged promptly" | High | Specify test method, frequency, flag recipient and deadline (e.g. within 24 h). | Open |
| D21 | S9, S10 | Incompleteness | Regeneration needs the box withdrawn, but S10 and S4 are not reconciled; no regeneration process exists. | "flagged... for regeneration" | High | Define a regeneration workflow with an exemption or counting rule for the S10 quota. | Open |
| D22 | S10 | Ambiguity | Base of the 20% (start of year, current count), year definition and rounding are unclear (3 boxes x 20% = 0.6). | "at most 20% of its boxes of a species per year" | High | Define calendar year, base = count on 1 Jan, round down with a minimum of 1 box. | Open |
| D23 | S11 | Ambiguity | "Re-inspection" is not defined: who inspects, against what criteria, how long it takes. | "only after re-inspection" | Medium | Define the inspection checklist, inspector role and result record. | Open |
| D24 | S11 | Incompleteness | No outcome if re-inspection fails (reject, destroy, return to depositor). | "may be redeposited" | Medium | Add the failure path and its audit entry. | Open |
| D25 | S12 | Non-verifiability | "Good audit trail" is subjective and untestable. | "keep a good audit trail" | High | Rewrite: every action logged with who, what, when, box ID, outcome; log is tamper-evident. | Open |
| D26 | S12 | Incompleteness | No log content, retention period, access rights or integrity protection. | "of all actions" | Medium | Specify fields, retention period, read-only access and integrity protection. | Open |
| D27 | S1-S12 | Untraceability | No requirement has a rationale, source stakeholder, priority or link to design or tests. | The whole S1-S12 table | Medium | Add columns: source, rationale, priority, verification method. | Open |
| D28 | S4 | Incompleteness | "One species per box" and a unique box ID appear in the scenario but in no requirement, yet S10 depends on species. | S10 uses "species"; S4 only says "a box" | Medium | Add: every box has a unique ID and exactly one species, checked at deposit. | Open |

**Totals:** 28 defects. Cross-requirement defects: 8 (D04, D08, D10, D11, D13, D14, D18, D21). Key ones: **S4 vs S5 (D08)** and **S7 vs S8 (D14)**.
